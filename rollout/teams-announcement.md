**🚀 Maayasthra People is live: your new workspace for attendance, leave and tasks**

Hi team,

From today we are moving to **Maayasthra People**, one place for everything you used to chase across chats and sheets.

**Get started in 3 steps**
1. **Open people.maayasthra.com** on your phone or computer. Bookmark it, or add it to your home screen.
2. **Create your account.** Choose **Create account**, enter your work email and a password. We email you a link to confirm it.
3. **Open the link, then sign in.** A one-minute tour starts. After it, finish your profile (photo, phone number, emergency contact).

If it says your email is not in the staff list, message an admin.

**What you can do**
- ✅ **Check in and out.** Your hours are kept for you. At check-out, send a short end-of-day report of what you worked on.
- 🌴 **Leave.** Ask for leave, see your balance and follow the answer. **For the next few months, please request leave in both Outlook and the portal.** Later we will move to the portal alone, and we will tell you when.
- 📋 **Tasks.** Your own tasks live in the Task app.
- 🧾 **Payslips.** Coming soon. It shows greyed out in the menu for now, and we will tell you when you can request one.
- 📣 **Announcements and calendar.** Company news, holidays, birthdays and who is on leave.

**Coming next** (no dates yet): leave in one place, installing it like an app on your phone, and a monthly summary of your hours.

**Good to know**
- Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. We email you a 6-digit code.
- Stuck? Ask an admin and tell them the version number under **About**.

The short guide, with screenshots, is attached. Please sign in and finish your profile this week.

Thank you! 🧡
