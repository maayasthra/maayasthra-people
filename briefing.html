Team guide

Maayasthra People

One place for your day at Maayasthra: check in and out, your tasks, announcements, the calendar and your hours. It works on a laptop and on your phone.

people.maayasthra.com

Getting started

  1. Sign in with your work emailUse the email the company has on file for you. If you forget your password, choose "Forgot password?" and a 6-digit code is emailed to you. It works once and lasts 10 minutes.
  2. Take the short tourThe first time you sign in, a one-minute tour shows you around. You can skip it, and run it again any time from your photo menu, top right.
  3. Finish your profileThe Home page shows a progress card until you have added a photo, a line about you, your phone number and an emergency contact (name, relationship and phone).

What you do every day

Start of day

Check in

Press Check in at the top when you start. Your worked time counts up from there. Press Check out when you finish.

Through the day

Your tasks

Open Task, then My Tasks. Cards move from Pending to In progress to Completed. Press Start on what you are working on. You can have several in progress at once. Submitted work shows your past day reports by day, week or month.

End of day

Report before you check out

Employees write a short report of the day: project, what you did, and the time as hours and minutes. It saves as a draft while you type, so nothing is lost if you click away.

Helpful things to know

The bell

The bell at the top tells you when a task is given to you. Employees also get a reminder 30 minutes and 15 minutes before their shift ends if the day's report is not sent yet.

Announcements

A new announcement pops up during your working hours with a chime, and repeats every minute until you press Got it. Admins can see who has read each one.

Light or dark

Open your photo menu and pick light, dark, or match your device. Your choice is remembered.

Retainer tasks

Retainer tasks have a blue tint and keep it wherever they are moved, so they are easy to spot.
